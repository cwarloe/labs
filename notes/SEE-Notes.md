Some of the TOMs were not well constructed and did not adequately map out the data sources and/or use the data sources/field names in the queries. ## SEEs

- Watch as much of the activities as possible but without distracting them. Perhaps we can mirror their screens or login to the consoles they are looking at real-time while in the fishbowl, then formulate specific questions before going back to their desks to shoulder surf?
- Don't have conversations with other SEEs or S/E team (or random visitors) in front of the evaluatees, go to the fishbowl or the S/E office and close the door. Prevents participants from being distracted or discouraged, and keeps them from using information in those conversations during the exercise.
- Only one SEE should be working with any participant at any one time (preferably, the actual SEE who is doing the eval on them)

## S/E team

- Come up with a general list of questions to ask operators that get at each of the graded areas - try to keep them open-ended and non-leading questions that will help determine their thought process and help determine whether they have an understanding of the tasks they are performing

## Crew Lead - Tips and Suggestions

- Let team members know that they can work as a team even though they are being evaluated individually
- Ensure that they are staying focused on using the queries/analytics that they developed for their TOM as the starting point
  - Ask them which TT they are working on and how they know that it's complete - did they measure using the MOP
  - Ask them to look at the TO which their TTs support and determine if
- Ensure that operators are calling out PAAs they are performing and getting approval BEFORE executing PCAs
- Compile the stick notes so that you have a timeline before the end of the exercise / prior to the MP outbrief and the internally focused debrief

## MQT

- Ensure that the operators see the link between the TOM development and their initial hunt
- Ensure that the operators are using the alert triage flowchart rather than just randomly looking through alerts
- Ensure that NA and HA understand that Suricata alerts are going to be based on network traffic, so most of the alerts are for the NA and HA should only be targeting specific alerts that have host data in them
- Ensure that both NA and HA have the ability to use the field mapping guide
